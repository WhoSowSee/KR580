Export (Ctrl+E) creates TXT or XLSX from the current processor state.

Each XLSX page or TXT section can select its own RAM range, registers, and flags. XLSX also supports column selection and stores pages as sheets in one workbook. TXT uses address=value lines and stores sections as named blocks in one file.

Export does not change the session. The result can later be imported as a whole, by sheet, or by section.
